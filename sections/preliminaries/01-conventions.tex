\subsection{Conventions}
\label{subsec:prelim-conventions}

\subsubsection*{$(\infty,2)$-categories}
$\Cat$ is the $\infty$-category of small $(\infty,1)$-categories and $\Spc \subset \Cat$ the full subcategory of spaces.
An \emph{$(\infty,2)$-category} is a $\Cat$-enriched $\infty$-category in the sense of Gepner--Haugseng \cite{gepnerhaugseng2015enriched}; $\twoCat$ is the $\infty$-category of small ones.
For objects $x,y$ of $\A$ we write $\Hom_{\A}(x,y) \in \Cat$ for the hom-category and $\mapp_{\A}(x,y) = \Hom_{\A}(x,y)^{\simeq}$ for the mapping space of the underlying $\infty$-category $\iota_{1}\A$.
Via $\Spc \subset \Cat$, every $\infty$-category, e.g.\ $\Delta^{n}$, is an $(\infty,2)$-category. The \emph{walking $2$-cell} $\wtc$ has objects $0,1$ and $\Hom(0,1) = \Delta^{1}$, $\Hom(1,0) = \emptyset$, $\Hom(0,0) = \Hom(1,1) = \mathrm{pt}$; $\bwtc \subset \wtc$ is the sub-$(\infty,2)$-category with $\Hom(0,1) = \partial\Delta^{1}$.

$\twoCat$ is presentable and cartesian closed, with internal hom $\func(\A,\B)$.

Its objects are functors, its $1$-morphisms $\Delta^{1} \to \func(\A,\B)$ are \emph{natural transformations} (naturality squares commute up to \emph{invertible} $2$-morphisms), and its $2$-morphisms are modifications.
The involutions $(-)\opcat$ and $(-)^{\mathrm{co}}$ of $\twoCat$ reverse $1$-morphisms, resp.\ $2$-morphisms.

\subsubsection*{The homotopy $2$-category}
The functor $h\colon \Cat \to \Cat_{\leq 1}$ preserves finite products; changing enrichment along it \cite{gepnerhaugseng2015enriched} gives the \emph{homotopy $2$-category} $\htwo\A$, a $(2,2)$-category, which we regard as a bicategory.
\todo{Reference for $\Cat_{\leq 1}$-enriched $\infty$-categories $=$ bicategories.}
It has the objects and $1$-morphisms of $\A$, and equivalence classes of $2$-morphisms of $\A$ as $2$-morphisms. Functors $\A \to \B$ induce pseudofunctors $\htwo\A \to \htwo\B$.

\begin{defi}
\label{def:adjunction}
An \emph{adjunction} $l \dashv r$ in $\A$ is an adjunction in $\htwo\A$: $1$-morphisms $l\colon x \to y$, $r\colon y \to x$ and $2$-morphisms $\eta\colon \mathrm{id}_{x} \Rightarrow rl$, $\varepsilon\colon lr \Rightarrow \mathrm{id}_{y}$ whose classes satisfy the triangle identities. A \emph{left adjoint} is a $1$-morphism that is part of an adjunction.
\end{defi}

\textbf{Notation.} For a $1$-morphism $f\colon x \to y$ of $\A$ and $t \in \A$ we write
\[
f_{\natural} := f \circ -\colon \Hom_{\A}(t,x) \to \Hom_{\A}(t,y) \ , \qquad f^{\natural} := - \circ f\colon \Hom_{\A}(y,t) \to \Hom_{\A}(x,t)
\]
for postcomposition and precomposition, and $(-)^{L}$, $(-)^{R}$ for left and right adjoints, e.g.\ $(f^{\natural})^{R}$. The notation $(-)^{*}$, $(-)_{*}$, $(-)_{!}$ is reserved for restriction along functors of $(\infty,2)$-categories, such as the restriction $\ell^{*}\colon \func(\Adj,\E) \to \func(\Delta^{1},\E)$ that will appear in \cref{subsec:mates-lemma}, and for Kan extensions.

Functors preserve adjunctions. In particular, for $t \in \A$ an adjunction $l \dashv r$ induces adjunctions of $\infty$-categories \cite{riehlverity2015twocategory}
\[
l_{\natural} \dashv r_{\natural}\colon \Hom_{\A}(t,x) \rightleftarrows \Hom_{\A}(t,y) , \qquad r^{\natural} \dashv l^{\natural}\colon \Hom_{\A}(x,t) \rightleftarrows \Hom_{\A}(y,t) \ .
\]

\subsubsection*{What is imported from $2$-category theory}
Arguments asserting that certain $2$-morphisms exist, or that two composites of $2$-morphisms agree (triangle identities, mate computations, interchange), take place in $\htwo\A$ and are imported verbatim from $2$-category theory.
Existence or uniqueness of homotopy-coherent data (functors out of the walking adjunction, natural transformations, monads) is only ever deduced from the inputs (I1)--(I4) that we will collect in \cref{subsec:prelim-inputs}. In particular we never lift an adjunction from $\htwo\A$ to $\A$ by hand.

\subsubsection*{Monomorphisms and locally full sub-$(\infty,2)$-categories}
A functor $G\colon \A \to \B$ is \emph{locally fully faithful} if every $\Hom_{\A}(x,x') \to \Hom_{\B}(Gx,Gx')$ is fully faithful, and a \emph{monomorphism} if every $\mapp_{\twoCat}(K,\A) \to \mapp_{\twoCat}(K,\B)$ is a monomorphism of spaces.
Since $\twoCat$ is generated under colimits by $\Delta^{0}$, $\Delta^{1}$, $\wtc$, and limits of monomorphisms of spaces are monomorphisms, it suffices to test these $K$.

A \emph{locally full sub-$(\infty,2)$-category} of $\B$ is a locally fully faithful monomorphism $G$; equivalently, a monomorphism $G$ such that
\[
\mapp(\wtc,\A) \longrightarrow \mapp(\wtc,\B) \times_{\mapp(\bwtc,\B)} \mapp(\bwtc,\A)
\]
is an equivalence. It is determined by a class of objects and a class of $1$-morphisms of $\B$, closed under equivalence and composition and containing the identities.
