\section{Two-dimensional localizations}
\label{sec:2dloc}

\Cref{subsec:2dloc-adjoining,subsec:2dloc-local,subsec:2dloc-plain} work in an arbitrary $(\infty,2)$-category; where a construction needs a colimit (cylinders, pushouts), its existence is a hypothesis. Presentability enters only in \cref{subsec:2dloc-presentable}, to construct a left adjoint to the inclusion of the local objects and to identify them with a pushout.
