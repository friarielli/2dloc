\subsection{Recollection: Bousfield localization as a pushout}
\label{subsec:2dloc-bousfield}

We recall the one-dimensional situation that we categorify.
Let $\Edge_{1} := \func(\Delta^{1},\Spc) \simeq \Free(\Delta^{1})$ (presheaves of spaces on $\Delta^{1} \simeq (\Delta^{1})\opcat$) be the free presentable $\infty$-category on an arrow, so $\func^{L}(\Edge_{1},\C) \simeq \func(\Delta^{1},\C)$ for $\C \in \prl$. The free presentable $\infty$-category on an \emph{equivalence} is $\Eqc := \Spc$, since the walking equivalence is contractible: being an equivalence is a property.
The functor $\Delta^{1} \to \mathrm{pt}$ induces $e_{!}\colon \Edge_{1} \to \Eqc$, $(A \to B) \mapsto B$, with fully faithful right adjoint $e^{*}\colon B \mapsto (B = B)$.

\begin{prop}
\label{prop:bousfield-pushout}
Let $\C \in \prl$ and let $S$ be a set of morphisms of $\C$, viewed as a map $S\colon \bigoplus_{S}\Edge_{1} \to \C$ in $\prl$ ($\bigoplus$ the coproduct in $\prl$).
\begin{enumerate}
\item The pushout
\[
\C[S^{-1}] := \C \sqcup_{\bigoplus_{S}\Edge_{1}} \textstyle\bigoplus_{S}\Eqc
\]
in $\prl$ satisfies $\func^{L}(\C[S^{-1}],\D) \simeq \{F \in \func^{L}(\C,\D) : F(s) \text{ invertible for all } s \in S\}$.
\item Passing to right adjoints, $\C[S^{-1}] \simeq \C \times_{\prod_{S}\Edge_{1}} \prod_{S}\Spc$ in $\Cat$ is the full subcategory of \emph{$S$-local} objects, those $c$ for which $s^{*}\colon \mapp_{\C}(y,c) \to \mapp_{\C}(x,c)$ is an equivalence for every $(s\colon x \to y) \in S$. The inclusion $\iota$ is right adjoint to the localization $L\colon \C \to \C[S^{-1}]$.
\item $T := \iota L$ is an idempotent monad on $\C$ and $\C[S^{-1}] \simeq \Alg_{T}(\C)$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{prop}

\begin{proof}
(1) holds since $\func^{L}(\Edge_{1},\D) \simeq \func(\Delta^{1},\D)$ and $\func^{L}(\Eqc,\D) \simeq \D$ is the full subcategory of equivalences.
(2) Under $\prl \simeq (\prr)\opcat$ \cite[5.5.3.4]{htt} the pushout becomes a pullback in $\prr$, computed in $\widehat{\Cat}$ \cite[5.5.3.18]{htt}. The right adjoint of $s\colon \Edge_{1} \to \C$ is $c \mapsto (s^{*}\colon \mapp(y,c) \to \mapp(x,c))$, and $\Spc \subset \Edge_{1}$ is the full subcategory of equivalences. The left adjoint $L$ exists by the adjoint functor theorem.
(3) As $\iota$ is fully faithful, the counit $L\iota \Rightarrow \mathrm{id}$ and hence $\mu = \iota\varepsilon L$ are equivalences; a fully faithful right adjoint is monadic.
\end{proof}

The categorification replaces the walking equivalence (contractible) by the walking reflection and coreflection of \cref{subsec:mates-lemma} (not contractible, but being reflective or coreflective is still a property by \cref{lem:mates-refl}).
As a result the localization is only a \emph{locally full} subcategory, and the monad is not idempotent.
