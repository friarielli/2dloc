\subsection{The mate lemma}
\label{subsec:2dloc-mates}

The following lemma is the two-dimensional replacement for the statement that ``the walking equivalence is contractible''.
We formulate it for an arbitrary $(\infty,2)$-category $\E$.

\begin{lem}[mates]
\label{lem:mates}
Let $\E$ be an $(\infty,2)$-category. The restriction functor
\[
\ell^{*}\colon \func(\Adj,\E) \longrightarrow \func(\Delta^{1},\E)
\]
is \lff{}, and its image is the locally full sub-$(\infty,2)$-category of $\func(\Delta^{1},\E)$ on
\begin{itemize}
\item the objects $u\colon x \to y$ which are left adjoints in $\E$;
\item the $1$-morphisms $(f_{0},f_{1},\varphi)\colon u \to u'$, $\varphi\colon u'f_{0} \simeq f_{1}u$, whose mate $\bar\varphi\colon f_{0}u^{R} \Rightarrow u^{\prime R}f_{1}$ is invertible (\emph{Beck--Chevalley squares}).
\end{itemize}
In particular $\ell^{*}$ is a monomorphism of $\infty$-categories (its fibres are empty or contractible).
\end{lem}

Recall that the mate of $\varphi$ is the composite
$f_{0}u^{R} \xRightarrow{\eta' f_{0}u^{R}} u^{\prime R}u'f_{0}u^{R} \xRightarrow{u^{\prime R}\varphi u^{R}} u^{\prime R}f_{1}uu^{R} \xRightarrow{u^{\prime R}f_{1}\varepsilon} u^{\prime R}f_{1}$, and that mates are compatible with pasting of squares and with $2$-cells (Kelly--Street \cite{kellystreet1974review}).

\begin{proof}
On objects the statement is \cref{lem:adj-contractible}.
Fix adjunctions $F = (u \dashv u^{R})$ and $F' = (u' \dashv u^{\prime R})$ in $\E$.
A $1$-morphism $F \to F'$ in $\func(\Adj,\E)$ consists of $f_{0}\colon x \to x'$, $f_{1}\colon y \to y'$, invertible $2$-cells $\varphi\colon u'f_{0} \simeq f_{1}u$ and $\psi\colon f_{0}u^{R} \simeq u^{\prime R}f_{1}$, and compatibilities with $\eta,\varepsilon$ (naturality with respect to the $2$-cells of $\Adj$, which are generated by $\eta$ and $\varepsilon$).
For fixed $(f_{0},f_{1},\varphi)$, the mate correspondence identifies the category of $2$-cells $f_{0}u^{R} \Rightarrow u^{\prime R}f_{1}$ with the category of $2$-cells $u'f_{0} \Rightarrow f_{1}u$; at the level of hom-categories this is the chain of equivalences
\[
\Hom(f_{0}u^{R},u^{\prime R}f_{1}) \simeq \Hom(u'f_{0}u^{R},f_{1}) \simeq \Hom(u'f_{0},f_{1}u)
\]
obtained from $u' \dashv u^{\prime R}$ and $u \dashv u^{R}$ by precomposition and postcomposition (adjunctions in $\E$ induce adjunctions on hom-categories, \cref{subsec:2dloc-conventions}), and the compatibility of $\psi$ with $\eta$ and $\varepsilon$ says precisely that $\psi$ corresponds to $\varphi$ under this equivalence, i.e.\ $\psi = \bar\varphi$.
Hence $\psi$ exists if and only if $\bar\varphi$ is invertible, and then it is unique; this identifies the essential image on $1$-morphisms and shows that $\ell^{*}$ is essentially surjective onto it on the hom-categories.
A $2$-morphism between two such $1$-morphisms is a modification $(m_{0}\colon f_{0} \Rightarrow g_{0}, m_{1}\colon f_{1} \Rightarrow g_{1})$ compatible with $\varphi,\varphi'$ and with $\psi,\psi'$. Since mating is natural in $2$-cells, compatibility with the $\psi$'s follows from compatibility with the $\varphi$'s, so $\ell^{*}$ is fully faithful on hom-categories.
\end{proof}

\begin{rmk}
\label{rmk:lax-epi}
Following \cite{adamekelbashirsobralvelebil2001laxepi}, call a functor $j\colon \A \to \B$ of $(\infty,2)$-categories a \emph{lax epimorphism} if $j^{*}\colon \func(\B,\E) \to \func(\A,\E)$ is \lff{} for every $\E$. \Cref{lem:mates} says that $\ell\colon \Delta^{1} \to \Adj$ is a lax epimorphism but not an epimorphism (in $\twoCat$): $j$ is an epimorphism if and only if $j^{*}$ is fully faithful. The difference between the two is exactly the Beck--Chevalley condition on $1$-morphisms.
Since $\func(-,\E)$ turns pushouts into pullbacks and \lff{} functors are stable under pullback, the pushout $\A \to \A\radj{S}$ of \cref{def:adjoin-right} is again a lax epimorphism, and so is $L\colon \D \to \D\radj{S}$ for the $2$-categories $\func^{L}_{\Cat}(-,\E)$.
\end{rmk}
