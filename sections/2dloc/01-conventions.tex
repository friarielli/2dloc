\subsection{Conventions}
\label{subsec:2dloc-conventions}

Throughout, $\Cat$ denotes the $\infty$-category of small $(\infty,1)$-categories and $\Spc \subset \Cat$ the full subcategory of spaces.
An \emph{$(\infty,2)$-category} is a $\Cat$-enriched $\infty$-category in the sense of Gepner--Haugseng \cite{gepnerhaugseng2015enriched}; we write $\twoCat$ for the $\infty$-category of small $(\infty,2)$-categories, and for an $(\infty,2)$-category $\A$ and objects $x,y$ we write $\Hom_{\A}(x,y) \in \Cat$ for the hom-category.
Every $(\infty,2)$-category has an underlying $(\infty,1)$-category with the same objects and mapping spaces $\mapp_{\A}(x,y) = \Hom_{\A}(x,y)^{\simeq}$.
Enriched functors, enriched natural transformations and enriched presheaves are the ones of \cite{gepnerhaugseng2015enriched,hinich2020yoneda,heine2023enriched}; for $(\infty,2)$-categories $\A$, $\B$ we write $\func(\A,\B)$ for the $(\infty,2)$-category of enriched functors, whose $1$-morphisms are the enriched natural transformations (their component squares commute up to \emph{invertible} $2$-cells).
The involution $(-)\opcat$ on $\twoCat$ reverses $1$-morphisms and $(-)^{\mathrm{co}}$ reverses $2$-morphisms.

Most of the arguments below are $2$-categorical: they use only composition, whiskering and pasting of $2$-cells in hom-categories. We import such arguments from strict $2$-categories to $(\infty,2)$-categories without further comment; the only genuinely homotopy-coherent input is the contractibility of the space of adjunction data (\cref{lem:adj-contractible}), which we take from the literature.

\subsubsection*{Adjunctions}
An \emph{adjunction} in an $(\infty,2)$-category $\A$ is a pair of $1$-morphisms $l\colon x \to y$, $r\colon y \to x$ together with $2$-cells $\eta\colon \mathrm{id}_x \Rightarrow rl$ and $\varepsilon\colon lr \Rightarrow \mathrm{id}_y$ satisfying the two triangle identities up to equivalence; we write $l \dashv r$.
A $1$-morphism $l$ is a \emph{left adjoint} if it is part of an adjunction.
By the enriched Yoneda lemma this is a representable notion: $l$ is a left adjoint in $\A$ if and only if for every object $t$ the functor $l_{*}\colon \Hom_{\A}(t,x) \to \Hom_{\A}(t,y)$ is a left adjoint of $\infty$-categories and the induced squares are Beck--Chevalley for every $t' \to t$; equivalently, if and only if $l^{*}\colon \Hom_{\A}(y,t) \to \Hom_{\A}(x,t)$ is a \emph{right} adjoint for every $t$, compatibly with precomposition.

\begin{lem}[Riehl--Verity]
\label{lem:adj-contractible}
Let $l\colon x \to y$ be a $1$-morphism in an $(\infty,2)$-category $\A$. The space of adjunction data $(r,\eta,\varepsilon,\dots)$ exhibiting $l$ as a left adjoint is either empty or contractible.
\end{lem}

This is \cite[Thm.~4.4.18]{riehlverity2016adjunctions} for $\A = \Cat$ and, by the same representability argument, for any $(\infty,2)$-category; see also \cite{haugseng2021laxmonads} and \cite{abellangagnahaugseng2024straightening}.

Concretely, \cref{lem:adj-contractible} says that the \emph{walking adjunction} $\Adj$ of \cite{riehlverity2016adjunctions} (the $2$-category freely generated by an adjunction $l \dashv r$ between two objects $-$ and $+$, viewed as an $(\infty,2)$-category) has the following universal property: the restriction functor
\[
\ell^{*}\colon \func(\Adj,\A) \longrightarrow \func(\Delta^{1},\A), \qquad \ell\colon \Delta^{1} \to \Adj,\ (0 \to 1) \mapsto (l\colon - \to +),
\]
is, on underlying $\infty$-categories, a monomorphism whose essential image consists of the left adjoints.
We also write $\rho\colon \Delta^{1} \to \Adj$ for the functor selecting $r\colon + \to -$.
The duality $\Adj\opcat \simeq \Adj$ exchanges $\ell$ and $\rho$ (up to the identification $(\Delta^{1})\opcat \simeq \Delta^{1}$), and $\Adj^{\mathrm{co}} \simeq \Adj$ does the same.

\subsubsection*{Presentable $(\infty,2)$-categories}
We take a \emph{presentable $(\infty,2)$-category} to be a $\Cat$-module in $\prl$, and set
\[
\tprl := \modcat{\Cat}{\prl},
\]
the $\infty$-category of presentable $\infty$-categories equipped with a colimit-preserving action of $\Cat$ (for the cartesian product), and $\Cat$-linear left adjoints between them.
By \cite[\S 7]{gepnerhaugseng2015enriched} every object $\D \in \tprl$ is canonically $\Cat$-enriched, with $\func(K,\Hom_{\D}(d,d')) \simeq \mapp_{\D}(K \otimes d, d')$, and every $\Cat$-linear left adjoint is canonically an enriched functor. We regard $\D$ as an $(\infty,2)$-category in this way; it is tensored and cotensored over $\Cat$.
An object $\D \in \tprl$ has all (small) enriched colimits, and an enriched functor between objects of $\tprl$ is in $\tprl$ if and only if it preserves them.

For a small $(\infty,2)$-category $\A$ the enriched presheaf category
\[
\Free(\A) := \func(\A\opcat,\Cat)
\]
is in $\tprl$, and it is the free presentable $(\infty,2)$-category on $\A$: by the enriched Yoneda lemma \cite{hinich2020yoneda,heine2023enriched} restriction along the Yoneda embedding $y\colon \A \to \Free(\A)$ is an equivalence of $(\infty,2)$-categories
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:free-univ}
\func^{L}_{\Cat}\bigl(\Free(\A),\D\bigr) \xrightarrow{\ \simeq\ } \func(\A,\D)
\end{equation}
for every $\D \in \tprl$, where the left-hand side is the $(\infty,2)$-category of $\Cat$-linear left adjoints.

A functor $f\colon \A \to \B$ of small $(\infty,2)$-categories induces $\Free(f) = f_{!}\colon \Free(\A) \to \Free(\B)$ (enriched left Kan extension) with right adjoint the restriction $f^{*}$.
The functor $\Free\colon \twoCat \to \tprl$ preserves colimits, being left adjoint to the forgetful functor (up to size).

We write $\Edge := \Free(\Delta^{1})$ and $\Free(\Adj)$ for the free presentable $(\infty,2)$-categories on an arrow and on an adjunction.
By \eqref{eq:free-univ}, a $1$-morphism $s\colon x \to y$ in $\D \in \tprl$ is the same thing as a map $s\colon \Edge \to \D$ in $\tprl$, and an adjunction in $\D$ is the same thing as a map $\Free(\Adj) \to \D$.
