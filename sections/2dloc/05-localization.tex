\subsection{The localization inside the original category}
\label{subsec:2dloc-local}

Fix $\D \in \tprl$ and a set $S$ of $1$-morphisms of $\D$.
For $(s\colon x \to y) \in S$ and $d \in \D$ write $s^{*} := \Hom_{\D}(s,d)\colon \Hom_{\D}(y,d) \to \Hom_{\D}(x,d)$.

\begin{defi}
\label{def:S-local}
An object $d \in \D$ is \emph{$S$-local} if $s^{*}$ admits a left adjoint $s_{!}$ for every $s \in S$.
A $1$-morphism $f\colon d \to d'$ between $S$-local objects is \emph{$S$-local} if for every $s \in S$ the (strictly commuting) square
\[
\begin{tikzcd}[column sep=large]
\Hom_{\D}(y,d) \ar[r, "f_{*}"] \ar[d, "s^{*}"'] & \Hom_{\D}(y,d') \ar[d, "s^{*}"] \\
\Hom_{\D}(x,d) \ar[r, "f_{*}"'] & \Hom_{\D}(x,d')
\end{tikzcd}
\]
satisfies the Beck--Chevalley condition, i.e.\ the mate $s_{!}f_{*} \Rightarrow f_{*}s_{!}$ is invertible.
We write $\Pc_{S} \subset \D$ for the locally full sub-$(\infty,2)$-category on the $S$-local objects and $S$-local $1$-morphisms (all $2$-morphisms between $S$-local $1$-morphisms are allowed), and $\iota\colon \Pc_{S} \hookrightarrow \D$ for the inclusion.
\end{defi}

Note that $\Pc_{S}$ is indeed a sub-$(\infty,2)$-category: identities are $S$-local, and mates are compatible with pasting \cite{kellystreet1974review}, so $S$-local morphisms are closed under composition.

\begin{thm}
\label{thm:2dloc-local}
Let $\D \in \tprl$ and let $S$ be a set of $1$-morphisms of $\D$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The functor $L\colon \D \to \D\radj{S}$ admits a right adjoint, which is \lff{} with essential image $\Pc_{S}$. Hence $\D\radj{S} \simeq \Pc_{S}$ as $(\infty,2)$-categories: the localization is realized inside $\D$ as the locally full sub-$(\infty,2)$-category of $S$-local objects and $S$-local morphisms, and $L \dashv \iota$.
\item $T := \iota L$ is a $\Cat$-enriched monad on $\D$, the inclusion $\iota$ is monadic, and so $\Pc_{S} \simeq \Alg_{T}(\D)$. Moreover $T$-algebra structures are \emph{properties}: for every $d \in \D$ the space of $T$-algebra structures on $d$ is empty or contractible, and it is non-empty if and only if $d$ is $S$-local.
\end{enumerate}
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
(1) \emph{The underlying $\infty$-category.}
Colimits in $\tprl = \modcat{\Cat}{\prl}$ are computed in $\prl$ \cite[4.2.3.5]{ha}\todo{check the tag: the forgetful functor from modules preserves colimits}.
As in the proof of \cref{prop:bousfield-pushout}, the pushout defining $\D\radj{S}$ is computed by passing to right adjoints and forming the pullback in $\widehat{\Cat}$ \cite[5.5.3.4, 5.5.3.18]{htt}.
The right adjoint of $s\colon \Edge \to \D$ is $d \mapsto \Hom_{\D}(s(-),d)$, i.e.\ the arrow $s^{*}\colon \Hom(y,d) \to \Hom(x,d)$ viewed as an object of $\Edge = \func(\Delta^{1},\Cat)$, and the right adjoint of $\Free(\ell)$ is $\ell^{*}$. Hence the underlying $\infty$-category of $\D\radj{S}$ is the pullback
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:pullback}
\D\radj{S} \simeq \D \times_{\prod_{S}\func(\Delta^{1},\Cat)} \textstyle\prod_{S}\func(\Adj,\Cat),
\end{equation}
and the projection to $\D$ is the right adjoint $\iota$ of $L$.
By \cref{lem:mates} each $\ell^{*}$ is a monomorphism whose image consists of the left adjoints and the Beck--Chevalley squares; monomorphisms are stable under products and pullbacks, so $\iota$ is a monomorphism of $\infty$-categories with image the $S$-local objects and $S$-local morphisms. This identifies the underlying $\infty$-category of $\D\radj{S}$ with that of $\Pc_{S}$.

\emph{The enrichment.}
Since $L$ is $\Cat$-linear, its right adjoint $\iota$ preserves cotensors: for $d \in \D$ and $p \in \D\radj{S}$, $\mapp_{\D}(d,\iota(K \pitchfork p)) \simeq \mapp(K \otimes Ld,p) \simeq \mapp(L(K \otimes d),p) \simeq \mapp_{\D}(K \otimes d,\iota p) \simeq \mapp_{\D}(d,K \pitchfork \iota p)$, naturally in $d$, so $\iota(K \pitchfork p) \simeq K \pitchfork \iota p$ by Yoneda.
For $q,p \in \D\radj{S}$ and $K \in \Cat$ we therefore get
\[
\func\bigl(K,\Hom_{\D\radj{S}}(q,p)\bigr)^{\simeq} \simeq \mapp_{\D\radj{S}}(q,K \pitchfork p) \subset \mapp_{\D}(\iota q,K \pitchfork \iota p) \simeq \func\bigl(K,\Hom_{\D}(\iota q,\iota p)\bigr)^{\simeq},
\]
where by the first part the left-hand side is the union of those components consisting of $S$-local morphisms $g\colon \iota q \to K \pitchfork \iota p$.
Now $\Hom_{\D}(s,K \pitchfork \iota p) = \func(K,\Hom_{\D}(s,\iota p))$ with left adjoint $\func(K,s_{!})$, and evaluation at $k \in K$ commutes with $s^{*}$, $s_{!}$ and their unit and counit. Hence the mate of the square of $g$ evaluates at $k$ to the mate of the square of the component $g_{k}\colon \iota q \to \iota p$, and $g$ is $S$-local if and only if every $g_{k}$ is, i.e.\ if and only if the functor $K \to \Hom_{\D}(\iota q,\iota p)$ corresponding to $g$ factors through the full subcategory of $S$-local morphisms.
Thus $\func(K,\Hom_{\D\radj{S}}(q,p))^{\simeq} \simeq \func(K,\Hom_{\Pc_{S}}(q,p))^{\simeq}$ naturally in $K$, and $\Hom_{\D\radj{S}}(q,p) \simeq \Hom_{\Pc_{S}}(q,p)$ by Yoneda. This proves that $\iota$ is \lff{} with image $\Pc_{S}$.

(2) The adjunction $L \dashv \iota$ is $\Cat$-enriched: $\func(K,\Hom_{\Pc_{S}}(Ld,p))^{\simeq} \simeq \mapp(K \otimes Ld,p) \simeq \mapp(K \otimes d,\iota p) \simeq \func(K,\Hom_{\D}(d,\iota p))^{\simeq}$ naturally in $K$, so $\Hom_{\Pc_{S}}(Ld,p) \simeq \Hom_{\D}(d,\iota p)$.
Any adjunction in the $(\infty,2)$-category of (large) $(\infty,2)$-categories induces a monad \cite{riehlverity2016adjunctions}, so $T = \iota L$ is a $\Cat$-enriched monad on $\D$ with unit $\eta\colon \mathrm{id} \Rightarrow \iota L$ and multiplication $\mu = \iota\varepsilon L$.

For monadicity we verify the hypotheses of the Barr--Beck--Lurie theorem \cite[4.7.3.5]{ha} for $\iota$ on underlying $\infty$-categories.
$\iota$ is conservative: if $f$ is $S$-local and invertible in $\D$, then the mate of the square of $f^{-1}$ is inverse to the mate of the square of $f$ (mates are compatible with pasting), hence invertible, so $f^{-1}$ is $S$-local.
$\iota$ preserves geometric realizations of $\iota$-split simplicial objects: let $q_{\bullet}$ be a simplicial object of $\Pc_{S}$ with $\iota q_{\bullet}$ split. Write $q_{\bullet} = (\iota q_{\bullet}, F_{\bullet})$ in the pullback \eqref{eq:pullback}, with $F_{\bullet} \in \prod_{S}\func(\Adj,\Cat)$ and $\ell^{*}F_{\bullet} \simeq \Hom_{\D}(s,\iota q_{\bullet})$. Split simplicial objects admit colimits preserved by every functor \cite[6.1.3.16]{htt}, so $|\iota q_{\bullet}|$ exists in $\D$ and $\Hom_{\D}(s,|\iota q_{\bullet}|) \simeq |\Hom_{\D}(s,\iota q_{\bullet})|$; on the other side $|F_{\bullet}|$ exists in $\func(\Adj,\Cat)$ and is preserved by $\ell^{*}$, since colimits there are computed pointwise. Hence $(|\iota q_{\bullet}|,|F_{\bullet}|)$ is a colimit of $q_{\bullet}$ in the pullback, and $\iota$ preserves it.
By \cite[4.7.3.5]{ha}, $\iota$ is monadic, i.e.\ $\Pc_{S} \simeq \Alg_{T}(\D)$ over $\D$.
Finally, under this equivalence the forgetful functor $\Alg_{T}(\D) \to \D$ is $\iota$, which is a monomorphism by (1); so its fibres, the spaces of $T$-algebra structures, are empty or contractible, and non-empty exactly over the $S$-local objects.
\end{proof}

\begin{rmk}
\label{rmk:eilenberg-moore-enriched}
\Cref{thm:2dloc-local}(2) identifies $\Pc_{S}$ with $\Alg_{T}(\D)$ as $\infty$-categories. The $(\infty,2)$-category of $T$-algebras (the Eilenberg--Moore object of $T$ in the $(\infty,2)$-category of $(\infty,2)$-categories) has hom-categories the equalizers of $\Hom_{\D}(d,d') \rightrightarrows \Hom_{\D}(Td,d')$, $f \mapsto f a$, $f \mapsto a' T(f)$; since $T$ will turn out to be lax-idempotent (\cref{thm:2dloc-kz}), these are again the full subcategories of $S$-local morphisms, so the identification holds at the level of $(\infty,2)$-categories as well.

\begin{rmk}
\label{rmk:2dloc-compare-bousfield}
Compare with \cref{prop:bousfield-pushout}: there the local objects are those $c$ for which $s^{*}$ is an \emph{equivalence}, here those for which $s^{*}$ is a \emph{right adjoint}.
The Beck--Chevalley condition on morphisms has no one-dimensional counterpart, because an inverse, unlike an adjoint, is preserved by every square.
This condition is exactly what prevents $\iota$ from being fully faithful, and hence $T$ from being idempotent: the monad $T$ remembers, on $\Hom(Td,d')$, the non-local morphisms as well.
\end{rmk}
